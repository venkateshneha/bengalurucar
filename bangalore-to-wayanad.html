Overview

Bangalore to Wayanad Cab Service — 3 Day Tour Package

Wayanad — Kerala's "Land of Paddy Fields" veiled in evergreen Western Ghats forest

Wayanad (Wayanadu), meaning "Land of Paddy Fields" in Malayalam, is one of Kerala's most enchanting highland districts — a contiguous swathe of evergreen forest, tribal heritage and dramatic natural scenery tucked into the upper reaches of the Western Ghats at elevations of 700–2,100 metres. Sharing borders with Karnataka and Tamil Nadu, Wayanad is both ecologically extraordinary and culturally rich, home to the indigenous Adivasi communities who have inhabited these forests for millennia, alongside tigers, elephants, leopards, Indian bison and over 300 species of birds.

Our Bangalore to Wayanad cab service makes this 3-day Kerala escape seamless from the moment you step out of your door. The journey begins with a scenic drive through Bandipur National Park — one of India's finest tiger reserves — where elephant herds frequently cross the road in the early morning hours. Day 2 in Wayanad is devoted to the district's most iconic natural landmarks: Banasura Sagar Dam, the largest earthen dam in India and Asia's second largest, the challenging trek up Chembra Peak with its famous heart-shaped lake, the serene Pookode Lake set amid a subtropical forest, and the extraordinary Kuruva Island — a protected river delta habitat accessible only by bamboo raft.

Day 3 brings the remarkable Edakkal Caves in the Ambukuthi Hills — two natural rock formations containing prehistoric petroglyphs dating back over 6,000 years, making them one of the oldest known examples of writing in South Asia. The return journey also passes through Muthanga Wildlife Sanctuary and optionally Tholpetty Wildlife Sanctuary, offering further chances of wildlife sightings before the drive back to Bangalore.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Bangalore to Wayanad — 3 Day Tour Itinerary

Your cab departs at 7:00 AM from Bangalore on Day 1. The route passes through Bandipur National Park on Day 1, full-day Wayanad sightseeing on Day 2, and Edakkal Caves plus wildlife sanctuaries on Day 3 before returning to Bangalore.

Day 01
Bangalore to Wayanad — via Bandipur National Park 5–6 Hours Drive · ~270 km
Overnight: Wayanad

Depart Bangalore at 7:00 AM and drive southwest through Mysore towards the Kerala border. The route passes through the magnificent Bandipur National Park — one of India's most important tiger reserves under Project Tiger — where the early morning drive through dense deciduous forest frequently rewards passengers with sightings of elephant herds, spotted deer, gaur (Indian bison) and the occasional leopard crossing. As you ascend the Nilgiri ghats towards Wayanad, the vegetation transforms dramatically into lush evergreen forest. Check in at your hotel in Kalpetta or Mananthavady by evening and relax in the cool highland air.

Day 02
Wayanad Full Day Sightseeing — Dam, Peak, Lake & Island Local Sightseeing · ~70 km within district
Overnight: Wayanad

After breakfast, set out for a full day of Wayanad's most celebrated landmarks. Begin at Banasura Sagar Dam — the largest earthen dam in India and Asia's second largest — where a series of small rocky islands emerge dramatically from the reservoir surface. Trek up to Chembra Peak (2,100 m) — Wayanad's highest point — for panoramic views over three states, passing the famous heart-shaped lake near the summit. The afternoon is spent at the tranquil Pookode Lake, a freshwater lake surrounded by subtropical forest, and the extraordinary Kuruva Island — a 950-acre riverine island ecosystem accessible only by bamboo raft through the Kabani river channels, home to rare orchids and river otters.

Day 03
Edakkal Caves & Wildlife Sanctuaries — Return to Bangalore Return Drive ~270 km
Return: Bangalore

After breakfast and hotel checkout, begin with a visit to the remarkable Edakkal Caves — two natural caves in the Ambukuthi Hills reached by a scenic 45-minute uphill walk through forest. The caves contain prehistoric petroglyphs (rock engravings) dating back over 6,000 years, believed to depict human figures and animals — one of the rarest archaeological finds in India. On the return drive, pass through Muthanga Wildlife Sanctuary (part of the Nilgiri Biosphere Reserve) and optionally the less-visited Tholpetty Wildlife Sanctuary in North Wayanad — both excellent for spotting elephants, sambar deer and birds before heading back to Bangalore.

Best Time to Visit Wayanad from Bangalore

Best Season October – February

Post-monsoon Wayanad is spectacularly green; cool temperatures (12–22°C), clear skies and excellent wildlife visibility in sanctuaries — the absolute best time to visit

Most Popular
Good Season March – May
