Overview

Bangalore to Hogenakkal Cab Service — 1 Day Tour Package

Hogenakkal Falls — "The Niagara of India" on the sacred Kaveri

Hogenakkal, meaning "smoking rocks" in Kannada, is one of South India's most spectacular waterfall destinations. Nestled on the banks of the sacred Kaveri river in the Dharmapuri district of Tamil Nadu, it is famous for its dramatic gorge landscape, mist-shrouded cascades and unique round-basket coracle boat rides through narrow rocky channels.

Located just 127 km from Bangalore, Hogenakkal is the perfect day-trip escape from the city bustle. Our Bangalore to Hogenakkal cab service gets you there in approximately 3 hours via the NH844, dropping you right at the waterfall entrance for a full day of nature and adventure before safely returning you home.

The falls are at their most magnificent between July and February when the Kaveri is in full flow, with multiple cascades tumbling over ancient black Precambrian rock formations — some of the oldest exposed rock on Earth. Beyond the main falls, the area is known for its fish spa and traditional oil bath facilities, the serene Pennagaram Reserve Forest and the tranquil Melagiri Hills as a backdrop.

Itinerary

1 Day Hogenakkal Tour Package — Day Itinerary

Your driver picks you up at 8:00 AM from your location in Bangalore. The itinerary below covers the full Bangalore to Hogenakkal one day tour package — approximately 300 km round trip in 12 hours.

8:00 AM

Departure from Bangalore Start Point

Your cab departs from your home, hotel or the Bangalore Airport. The drive to Hogenakkal takes approximately 3 hours via the NH844 through Hosur — a scenic route through the Eastern Ghats foothills and lush mango orchards of Tamil Nadu.

~11:00 AM

Arrival at Hogenakkal

Arrive at the Hogenakkal waterfall complex in Dharmapuri district, Tamil Nadu. Your driver will park at the designated parking area while you proceed to the main waterfall viewpoints and ghat areas. Entry tickets to the falls area are available at the ticket counter.

11:00 AM

Hogenakkal Waterfalls — Main Falls Viewpoint Must See

The main cascade at Hogenakkal is a breathtaking sight — the Kaveri river plunges through a narrow gorge of ancient black carbonatite rocks, throwing up a constant mist that gives the falls their name ("smoking rocks"). Multiple viewpoints along the ghat allow you to experience the falls from different angles.

11:30 AM

Coracle Boat Ride on the Kaveri Highlight

The iconic coracle boat ride is the highlight of any Hogenakkal visit. Skilled boatmen navigate these traditional round wicker-and-bitumen baskets through the dramatic rocky channels, past thundering mini-falls and hidden pools. The ride takes you under the falls themselves for a truly unforgettable, soaking experience. Note: Coracle rides are privately operated and charged separately at the site.

1:00 PM

Fish Spa & Traditional Oil Bath

Hogenakkal is famous throughout South India for its traditional Kaveri fish spa. Local masseurs offer a unique ayurvedic-style oil bath and massage session on the riverbanks, followed by a dip in the Kaveri's naturally treated waters. The fish spa — where small Doctor Fish (Garra rufa) nibble away dead skin — is a therapeutic and ticklish experience beloved by visitors.

2:00 PM

Lunch at Hogenakkal (Own Expense)

Savour the famous Hogenakkal speciality — freshly fried Kaveri river fish. Numerous small stalls and restaurants along the falls serve freshly caught mahseer, catfish and other river fish fried in local spices. This is a beloved local tradition that draws visitors from across Tamil Nadu and Karnataka. Lunch is not included in the package and is at your own expense.

3:00 PM

Exploration & Photography

Spend the afternoon exploring the broader falls complex at your own pace — walk the nature trails along the gorge rim, visit the smaller subsidiary falls that are often less crowded, or simply sit by the river and soak in the misty, forested landscape of the Pennagaram Reserve Forest that flanks the Kaveri here.

~4:30 PM

Departure & Return to Bangalore

Depart from Hogenakkal for the return drive to Bangalore. The return journey takes approximately 3 hours, bringing you back to your drop location in the city by 7:30–8:00 PM, well-rested and refreshed from a wonderful day by the falls.
