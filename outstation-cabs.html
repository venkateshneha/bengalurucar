Outstation Taxi from Bangalore

One Way & Round Trip Cabs from Bangalore

Book an outstation cab from Bangalore for weekend getaways, family trips, temple visits and business travel. We run both one-way and round-trip journeys across Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Kerala and Andhra Pradesh, with experienced highway drivers who know the routes well.

Pricing is per kilometre and depends on the vehicle you choose. Driver allowance (night bata), tolls, parking and interstate permits are explained before you confirm, so the final bill matches what you were told.

Popular outstation routes include Bangalore to Mysore, Bangalore to Coorg, Bangalore to Ooty, Bangalore to Wayanad, Bangalore to Chikmagalur and Bangalore to Hogenakkal, along with Tirupati, Chennai, Pondicherry, Hampi, Gokarna, Kodaikanal and Goa on request.
