Overview

One Day Bangalore Tour Package — Full Day Sightseeing

Bengaluru — India's Silicon Valley with a royal heritage

Bengaluru (Bangalore) is far more than India's technology capital — it is a city of extraordinary contrasts, where 18th-century palaces stand beside glass skyscrapers, where fragrant Lalbagh gardens bloom in the shadow of busy flyovers, and where centuries-old temples attract as many visitors as the city's celebrated breweries and malls.

Our One Day Bangalore Tour Package covers the city's 11 most iconic landmarks in a single, expertly paced 12-hour journey — starting at 7:00 AM and covering approximately 120 km across the city. Whether you are a first-time visitor, a business traveller with a free day, or a Bangalorean wanting to rediscover your city, this tour offers the perfect introduction to the Garden City.

From the grand Dravidian spires of Bull Temple — one of Bangalore's oldest — and the fragrant glass houses of Lalbagh, to the Indo-Tudor magnificence of Bangalore Palace and the sheer wonder of the HAL Aerospace Museum housing India's military aircraft, every stop on this tour tells a different chapter of Bangalore's remarkable story.

Your cab departs from your hotel, home or Bangalore Airport at 7:00 AM sharp. The early start ensures you beat the city traffic and make the most of the day across all 11 attractions.

~7:30 AM

Bull Temple & Dodda Ganapathi Temple

Begin with two of Bangalore's most ancient and sacred sites, both located in the Basavanagudi neighbourhood. The Bull Temple (Nandi Temple), built in the 16th century by Kempe Gowda I, houses a massive monolithic Nandi bull carved from a single granite rock — over 4.5 metres tall and 6 metres long. A short walk away, the Dodda Ganapathi Temple shelters one of India's largest Ganesha idols, also carved from a single granite block and adorned with 100 kg of butter offerings by devotees.

~8:30 AM

Tipu Sultan's Summer Palace

Built entirely of teak wood in 1791, this exquisite Indo-Islamic palace was the summer residence of Tipu Sultan — the legendary "Tiger of Mysore." Its ornate arched corridors, gilded frescoes and slender pillars are a masterwork of Mughal-influenced craftsmanship. The palace now functions as a heritage museum housing Tipu-era artefacts, weapons and paintings that bring 18th-century Mysore to vivid life.

~9:30 AM

Lalbagh Botanical Garden Must Visit

Spread across 240 acres in the heart of Bangalore, Lalbagh was laid out in the 18th century by Hyder Ali and expanded by Tipu Sultan and later the British. The garden houses over 1,854 species of plants including centuries-old trees, a famous glass house modelled on London's Crystal Palace, a serene lake, a rose garden and a lotus pond. The Lalbagh flower shows (held twice a year) are among India's most celebrated horticultural events.

~11:00 AM

Cubbon Park & Government Museum

Cubbon Park is Bangalore's green lung — a 300-acre Victorian-era park laid out in 1870 by Major General Richard Sankey. With its towering rain trees, winding pathways and heritage buildings, it is the perfect mid-morning stroll. Within the park grounds, the Government Museum (established 1886) is one of the oldest museums in India, housing an exceptional collection of ancient sculptures, coins, inscriptions and archaeological finds from across Karnataka.

~12:30 PM

Vidhana Soudha — Outside View

Drive past the magnificent Vidhana Soudha — Karnataka's state legislature and one of the most imposing government buildings in India. Built in 1956 in a distinctive Neo-Dravidian granite style by Chief Minister S. Nijalingappa, its massive granite facade, ornate domes and the famous inscription "Government Work Is God's Work" make it one of Bangalore's most iconic photo-stops. Note: public entry is not permitted; the outside view and surroundings are equally impressive.

~1:30 PM

Bangalore Palace Must Visit

Modelled after England's Windsor Castle, this stunning Indo-Saracenic palace was built in 1887 by the Wadiyar royal family of Mysore. Its Tudor-style arches, fortified towers, floral motifs and turreted walls make it one of South India's most impressive royal residences. The palace interiors house royal portraits, vintage furniture, ornamental tilework and exquisite woodwork — a window into the opulent lifestyle of Bangalore's aristocracy. The sprawling palace grounds also host concerts and cultural events throughout the year.

~3:00 PM

Shiva Temple Kempfort

Nestled within the Kempfort area near Old Airport Road, this serene Lord Shiva temple features a 65-foot monolithic Shivalinga — one of the tallest in Karnataka — visible from a considerable distance. The temple complex is beautifully maintained with lush gardens and a peaceful atmosphere that provides a calming contrast to the surrounding urban bustle. A spiritually significant and visually striking landmark.

~4:00 PM

HAL Aerospace Museum Highlight

Run by Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (HAL) — India's premier aerospace manufacturer — this world-class museum at Marathahalli showcases the remarkable story of Indian aviation. The sprawling outdoor and indoor exhibits include real fighter jets, helicopters, transport aircraft and aero engines used by the Indian Air Force and Navy — among them the HF-24 Marut, HAL Ajeet, MiG-21 and the iconic Gnome helicopter. Ideal for aviation enthusiasts, families and anyone curious about India's defence heritage.

~5:30 PM

ISKCON Temple Highlight

The ISKCON Temple Bangalore — formally the Sri Radha Krishna Temple — is one of the largest ISKCON temples in the world. Spread across 7 acres on Chord Road, it is an architectural marvel combining classical Dravidian temple architecture with modern construction. The main shrine houses deities of Radha Krishna, Rukmini Satyabhama and Sita Ram Lakshmana. The temple complex includes a magnificent gold-painted tower, a cultural hall, a prasad restaurant serving delicious sattvic meals, and a book shop. The evening aarti here is a truly mesmerising spiritual experience.

~7:00 PM

Return to Your Location

After the evening aarti at ISKCON, your cab returns you to your hotel, home or Bangalore Airport — concluding a full, memorable day exploring the best of Bengaluru. Approximate return time: 7:00 PM.
