Our Journey

15 Years of Moving Bangalore

From a small fleet to a South India travel partner — here's how we got here.

2010

Bengaluru Cab Rentals is Founded

Started with 5 vehicles and a simple commitment — provide Bangalore's travellers with reliable, chauffeur-driven cabs for airport transfers and local hire. The first booking was an airport run from Koramangala to KIAL.

2013

Fleet Expansion & Outstation Launch

Expanded to 30+ vehicles including Toyota Innova SUVs. Launched outstation cab services to Mysore, Coorg and Ooty — becoming one of the first dedicated outstation taxi operators in South Bangalore.

2016

Tour Packages & Pan-Karnataka Coverage

Launched curated tour packages to Hogenakkal, Chikmagalur, Wayanad, Tirupati and Goa. Grew coverage to 20+ destinations across Karnataka, Tamil Nadu and Kerala.

2019

10,000+ Customers & Premium Fleet

Crossed the milestone of 10,000 happy customers. Added Innova Crysta and New Innova Crysta to the fleet. Introduced Tempo Traveller services for large group travel.

2021

Post-Pandemic Relaunch with Enhanced Safety

Relaunched with enhanced sanitization protocols, GPS-tracked vehicles and strengthened 24/7 customer support. Introduced the WhatsApp booking channel that quickly became the preferred way for customers to book.

2025

50,000+ Customers & New Digital Experience

Crossed 50,000 happy customers. Launched a fully redesigned website with online booking, detailed tour pages and transparent pricing — making it easier than ever for travellers to plan their journey with us.
