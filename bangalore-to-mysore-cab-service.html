Bangalore to Mysore Cab Service — 1 Day Tour Package

Mysore Palace — UNESCO World Heritage Nominee

Mysore, also known as the "City of Palaces," is one of South India's most visited heritage destinations — and it's just 140 km from Bangalore. Our Bangalore to Mysore cab service makes it effortless to explore this royal city in a single day, with a comfortable AC vehicle and a professional driver at your service.

The iconic Mysore Palace was built in 1912 for the 24th ruler of the Wodeyar Dynasty and is among the largest palaces in India. Its construction was commissioned by Maharaja Krishnaraja Wadiyar IV — referred to as "Rajarishi" (Saintly King) by Mahatma Gandhi — and stands as a breathtaking example of Indo-Saracenic architecture blending Hindu, Mughal, Rajput and Gothic styles.

Beyond the palace, Mysore offers a treasure trove of history, spirituality and natural beauty — from the hilltop Chamundeshwari Temple and the fragrant KRS Dam & Brindavan Gardens to the serene Sri Ranganathaswamy Temple and the historic tombs at Srirangapatna. Our 1-day Mysore tour package covers all major highlights so you don't miss a thing.

1 Day Mysore Tour Package — Sightseeing Itinerary

Your driver will pick you up early morning from your location in Bangalore. Below are all the sightseeing spots covered in this 1 day Mysore tour package:

01

Sri Ranganathaswamy Temple

One of the most important Vaishnava temples in South India, located on the island of Srirangapatna. Dedicated to Lord Ranganatha, this temple is a spiritual marvel with intricate Dravidian architecture dating back to the 9th century.

02

Tombs of Tipu Sultan & Hyder Ali (Gumbaz)

The Gumbaz mausoleum at Srirangapatna houses the tombs of Hyder Ali and his son Tipu Sultan — the legendary "Tiger of Mysore." The ebony and ivory doors and exquisite tilework make this a historically significant site.

03

Tipu Summer Palace

An exquisite example of Indo-Islamic architecture, Tipu Sultan's summer retreat in Mysore is famous for its teakwood pillars, ornate frescoes and beautiful arched corridors — a window into the opulent life of the Mysore royals.

04

Chamundeshwari Temple & Nandi Statue

Perched atop Chamundi Hill at 1,065 metres, this ancient temple dedicated to Goddess Chamundeshwari offers panoramic views of Mysore city. The enormous 5-metre Nandi (sacred bull) statue carved from solid rock is a must-see on the way up.

05

Mahishasura Statue

The towering statue of the demon king Mahishasura, for whom Mysore (Mahishooru) is named, stands majestically at the foothills of Chamundi Hill. A popular photography spot offering a dramatic backdrop of the city.

06

Mysore Wax Museum

Home to over 45 highly realistic wax figures of historical personalities, Indian leaders and global celebrities, the Mysore Wax Museum is a fun and interactive experience for visitors of all ages.

07

Mysore Zoo (Sri Chamarajendra Zoological Gardens)

One of the oldest and most well-maintained zoos in India, established in 1892. Home to over 1,500 animals across 168 species, including white tigers, gorillas, giraffes and a large variety of exotic birds and reptiles.

08

Mysore Maharaja Palace Must Visit

The crown jewel of Mysore — a magnificent palace illuminated by nearly 100,000 light bulbs on Sunday evenings and public holidays. The palace houses incredible artworks, royal artefacts and ornate Durbar Hall. One of India's most visited monuments after the Taj Mahal.

09

St. Philomena's Church

One of the tallest churches in India, built in Neo-Gothic style in 1936. With its stunning twin spires rising 54 metres into the sky, it resembles the famous Cologne Cathedral in Germany and is a serene and architecturally magnificent landmark.

10

KRS Dam & Brindavan Gardens

The Krishnaraja Sagara Dam on the Cauvery river is an engineering marvel. The adjoining Brindavan Gardens are world-famous for their terraced lawns, fountains, rose garden and spectacular illuminated musical fountain shows in the evenings — a perfect end to a royal day.
