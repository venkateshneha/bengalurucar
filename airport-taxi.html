Kempegowda Airport Transfers

Bangalore Airport Taxi, Pickup & Drop 24/7

Our Bangalore airport taxi service covers pickups and drops at Kempegowda International Airport (KIA / BLR), Terminal 1 and Terminal 2, at any hour. You get a fixed fare agreed at booking, with no surge pricing at night or during peak hours.

For airport pickups, we track your flight and adjust the driver's arrival if your flight is early or delayed. Your driver waits at the arrival area with your name, helps with luggage and takes you straight to your home, hotel or office. For airport drops, we plan the pickup time around Bangalore traffic so you reach the terminal with time to spare.

The airport is roughly 35 to 50 km from most parts of the city, and the drive usually takes 45 minutes to 1.5 hours depending on traffic. Choose a sedan for up to 4 passengers, an Ertiga or Kia Carens for families, an Innova or Innova Crysta for extra luggage, or a Tempo Traveller for groups.
