Overview

Bangalore to Mysore, Ooty & Coorg — 6N/7D Tour Package

Three iconic South Indian destinations — one seamless 7-day journey

The Bangalore to Mysore, Ooty & Coorg 6 Night 7 Day tour package is one of the most beloved multi-destination road trips in South India — combining royal heritage, mountain serenity and coffee-country wilderness in a single, perfectly paced journey.

Mysore (Mysuru), the former capital of the Kingdom of Mysore from 1399 to 1947, dazzles with its opulent Wodeyar palace, fragrant Devaraja Market, ancient temples and the magnificent KRS Brindavan Gardens. Ooty (Udhagamandalam), founded as a British Raj summer retreat in the Nilgiri Hills, enchants with its sprawling botanical gardens, misty Doddabetta Peak and the legendary toy train railway. Coorg (Kodagu), Karnataka's famed "Scotland of India," seduces with its emerald coffee estates, thundering Abbey Falls, sacred Talakaveri and the rich heritage of the Kodava people.

Our cab service handles all inter-city transfers seamlessly — you simply enjoy the scenery as we drive you through Bandipur National Forest, over Nilgiri ghats and across misty Coorg valleys.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

6N/7D Mysore · Ooty · Coorg — Complete Tour Itinerary

A perfectly crafted 7-day road journey through South India's finest destinations. Pick up from Bangalore Airport or Railway Station on Day 1; return by 4 PM on Day 7.

Arrival & Bangalore Sightseeing Bangalore
Overnight: Bangalore

Arrive in Bangalore — picked up from the Airport or Railway Station and transferred to your hotel for freshen-up. Then embark on a full Bangalore city sightseeing tour covering the city's most celebrated landmarks.

Overnight: Ooty

After breakfast, check out and drive to Ooty through the scenic Bandipur National Forest — keep an eye out for wildlife along the way. Arrive in the Nilgiris and begin sightseeing in India's beloved hill station.

Day 04
Ooty & Coonoor Full Day Ooty · Coonoor

A full day exploring neighbouring Coonoor — famous for its tea estates and dramatic viewpoints — before returning to Ooty for its iconic Doddabetta Peak and the fascinating tea factory and museum.

Day 05
Ooty to Coorg — Talakaveri & Bhagamandala 6–7 Hours Drive
Overnight: Coorg

After breakfast, embark on the longest drive of the trip — through the lush Western Ghats to the coffee highlands of Coorg. En route, visit the sacred Talakaveri, birthplace of the Kaveri river, and the ancient Bhagamandala temple complex.

Day 06
Coorg Full Day Sightseeing Madikeri, Coorg
Overnight: Coorg

A leisurely day exploring Coorg's most iconic landmarks — from thundering Abbey Falls deep in the coffee forests to the gilded Tibetan Buddhist Temple and the serene Omkareshwara Temple by the lake.

Abbey Falls
Raja's Tomb (Gaddige)
Tibetan Golden Temple, Kushalnagar
Omkareshwara Temple
Day 07
Coorg to Bangalore — Departure ~5–6 Hours Drive
Arrive Bangalore ~4 PM

After a relaxed breakfast, check out from your Coorg hotel and begin the return journey to Bangalore. Your cab will drop you at Bangalore Airport, Railway Station or your home/hotel by approximately 4:00 PM, concluding a memorable 7-day South India road journey.
